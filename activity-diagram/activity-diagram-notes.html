1. Admin Role (admin/approve-reject-youth-registration.html)

Admin — Approve / Reject Youth Registration

Narrative Description: This activity diagram illustrates the administrative verification process performed by the System Administrator before a newly registered youth member is granted login access to the eKabataan Youth Portal. Upon registration, every youth account starts in a locked pending state. The Administrator inspects the applicant's personal profile, barangay residency, educational credentials, and uploaded School ID image, then either activates the account or rejects the registration with documented remarks—recording an immutable entry in the system audit log in both cases.
  1. Start & Navigation: The System Administrator signs in to the Executive Console and opens the Youth Profiles Directory (/admin/youth-profiles).
  2. Queue Filtering: The Administrator filters the list by Pending status or searches by the applicant's name/email, prompting the system to retrieve matching YouthProfile records along with their PSGC address and School ID attachment path.
  3. Credential & ID Inspection: The Administrator opens the Youth Profile Drawer and reviews the applicant's personal details, barangay address, school information, and uploaded School ID photo.
  4. Decision — Registration Valid & Complete?
    • If Valid (Approve): The Administrator clicks Approve. The system updates the record to status = approved and is_active = true, stamps approved_by and approved_at, logs youth_profile.approved in activity_logs, and unlocks the account so the youth can sign in at /youth-login.
    • If Invalid / Incomplete (Reject): The Administrator clicks Reject and encodes an optional rejection reason (up to 500 characters). The system sets status = rejected and is_active = false, stores the rejection_reason, logs youth_profile.rejected in activity_logs, and keeps portal login blocked while displaying the rejection notice to the applicant.
2. MYDO Staff Role (mydo-staff/approve-reject-youth-registration.html)

MYDO Staff — Approve / Reject Youth Registration

Narrative Description: This activity diagram depicts the day-to-day registration screening workflow carried out by MYDO Staff Officers. Staff verify whether youth registrants meet municipal age, barangay residency, and enrollment authenticity requirements using their uploaded School ID before enabling their Youth Portal account and unique YD-XXXX-XXXX reference code.
  1. Start & Queue Access: The MYDO Staff Officer signs in to the Staff Workspace and navigates to Youth Profiles (/staff/youth-profiles).
  2. Filter Pending Sign-Ups: Staff filter the directory to display Pending registrations, loading paginated youth profiles from StaffYouthProfileController.
  3. Drawer Verification: Staff open the StaffYouthProfileDrawer to examine the registrant's birth date/age, barangay residency, school/program details, and uploaded School ID image.
  4. Decision — Meets MYDO Eligibility & Valid ID?
    • If Approved: Staff confirm approval (POST /staff/youth-profiles/{id}/approve). The system sets status = approved and is_active = true, records the approving Staff ID and timestamp, writes a youth_profile.approved audit log, and enables the youth member to sign in with their YD-XXXX-XXXX reference code.
    • If Rejected: Staff select Reject and enter a clear explanation (POST /staff/youth-profiles/{id}/reject). The system updates status = rejected and is_active = false, saves the rejection reason, logs youth_profile.rejected, and prevents portal access until resolved.
3. MYDO Staff Role (mydo-staff/manage-scholarship-application.html)

MYDO Staff — Manage Scholarship Application

Narrative Description: This activity diagram models the end-to-end evaluation of online tertiary scholarship applications by MYDO Staff. It incorporates real-time Pusher alerts, digital verification of five academic/residency documents, a server-side guard preventing duplicate scholarship grants across programs, audit logging, and real-time WebSocket status notifications back to the Youth Scholar.
  1. Notification / Queue Entry: MYDO Staff either click an incoming real-time alert on the Staff Notification Bell or open Scholarship Applicants (/staff/applicants) and filter by status.
  2. Dossier Inspection: Staff open the applicant's dossier (/staff/applicants/{applicant}) and examine the student's previous-semester GWA, monthly family income, and uploaded digital requirements (Certificate of Enrollment, Certificate of Grades, School ID, Cedula, and PSA Birth Certificate).
  3. Decision — Evaluation Outcome:
    • Approve Path & Duplicate-Beneficiary Guard: Staff submit Approved. The system first checks whether the youth is already an approved beneficiary of another scholarship program. If a conflict exists, the system halts approval and displays a warning banner; otherwise, it updates status = approved, stamps reviewed_by and reviewed_at, and proceeds to notification.
    • Reject Path & Reason Validation: Staff select Rejected and encode a mandatory rejection_reason. If the reason is missing, the system prompts a validation error; once provided, it updates status = rejected along with the remarks and reviewer metadata.
  4. Audit & Real-Time Youth Notification: The system logs applicant_status_update in activity_logs, stores a ScholarshipApplicationStatusNotification in the database, and broadcasts ScholarshipApplicationStatusEvent via Pusher so the Youth Scholar immediately receives the decision on their live Notification Bell.
4. MYDO Staff Role (mydo-staff/manage-event-participant.html)

MYDO Staff — Manage Event Participant

Narrative Description: This activity diagram describes how MYDO Staff manage youth sign-ups for municipal events and activities, enforce venue capacity limits, release slots when rejecting or withdrawing participants, and transition confirmed participants into on-site QR or manual attendance verification.
  1. Open Participant Roster: Staff navigate to Events & Activities (/staff/events-activities) and click Manage Sign-ups for a target activity, loading the participant queue ordered with pending sign-ups first alongside live slot counters.
  2. Filter & Select Action: Staff filter sign-ups (All, Needs Review, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled) and choose an action for a participant:
    • Approve Pending Sign-up: The system checks whether confirmed reservations have already reached the activity's capacity. If full, approval is blocked with a 422 capacity alert; if slots remain, the registration is updated to status = approved, confirming the youth's slot and enabling their QR Event Pass (YD-XXXX-XXXX).
    • Reject Pending or Withdraw Approved Sign-up: Staff enter a mandatory actionable reason (minimum 5 characters). Once validated, the system sets status = rejected, saves the reason for the youth to view, and releases the reserved slot back to the activity's available capacity.
  3. Event-Day Check-In Continuation (Optional): From the roster, Staff can click Open Attendance Check-in (/attendance) to scan the participant's QR Event Pass or enter their reference code, invoking row-locked verification in ActivityAttendanceService to record verified attendance.
5. Youth Scholar Role (youth-scholar/join-activity.html)

Youth Scholar — Join Activity

Narrative Description: This activity diagram outlines the concurrency-safe registration flow when an approved Youth Scholar joins a municipal event or workshop. Using database row locking (lockForUpdate()), the system prevents overbooking of the last available seat and automatically routes the registration to either instant confirmation (with a QR Event Pass) or the MYDO Staff approval queue depending on the event's configuration.
  1. Browse Events Feed: The Youth Scholar signs in, opens Events & Activities (/youth/services/events-activities), and browses published upcoming events (while the system ensures the youth profile holds a unique YD-XXXX-XXXX reference code).
  2. Initiate Join & Concurrency Lock: The Youth clicks Join Activity (POST /join). Inside a database transaction, the system locks the Activity row via lockForUpdate() and checks if the youth already holds an active pending or approved registration.
  3. Sequential Eligibility & Capacity Guards:
    • Schedule & Publication Check: Verifies the activity is published, belongs to an active category, requires registration, has opened for registration, and has not yet closed or started.
    • Prior Rejection Check: Verifies the youth was not previously rejected for the same activity (otherwise instructing them to contact the MYDO office).
    • Capacity Check: Counts active slot-reserving registrations (pending + approved) against capacity. If any guard fails, the system returns a validation error banner.
  4. Decision — Requires Staff Approval?
    • If requires_approval = true: The system saves ActivityRegistration with status = pending, reserving the slot while displaying a Pending Review badge until Staff review.
    • If requires_approval = false (Auto-Confirm): The system saves ActivityRegistration with status = approved and immediately unlocks the Youth Scholar's QR Event Pass (YD-XXXX-XXXX) for venue check-in.
6. SK Official Role (sk/submit-document-report.html)

SK Official — Submit Document / Event Evidence Report

Narrative Description: This activity diagram captures how Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) Officials submit post-event photo documentation and narrative reports for published municipal activities. Submissions are validated for image authenticity and file size, atomically stored on disk, and linked to the SK Official's position and assigned Barangay for MYDO Staff and Admin monitoring.
  1. Select Published Event: The SK Official signs in to the SK Portal, opens Events & Evidence Upload (/sk/events-activities), searches or filters by category, and clicks Upload Event Evidence on the target event.
  2. Photo Selection & Format/Size Guard: The SK Official selects 1 to 10 event photos. Client and server checks enforce strictly PNG, JPG, or JPEG images up to 5MB per file. Invalid or oversized files are rejected with an error prompt; valid images are staged in storage/app/public/temp via POST /temp-upload.
  3. Narrative Entry & Server Verification: The SK Official writes the official event description/narrative (up to 2,000 characters) and clicks Submit (POST /sk/events-activities/{activity}/evidence). The controller verifies that all staged files exist on disk with valid image MIME types and that the narrative is non-empty.
  4. Atomic Storage & MYDO Visibility: Within a database transaction, staged images are moved to upfiles/sk-evidences/activity-{id}/sk-{user_id} and an SkActivityEvidence record is created. The submission is marked Submitted to MYDO and becomes immediately viewable in the Staff and Admin SK Evidence Modal tagged with the official's SK Position and Barangay.
7. SK Official Role (sk/view-sk-submit-status.html)

SK Official — View SK Submit Status

Narrative Description: This activity diagram shows how an SK Official tracks their barangay's documentation compliance across municipal events—checking overall submission KPIs on the SK Dashboard, inspecting per-event submission badges, reviewing previously uploaded photo galleries and narrative timestamps, or removing an erroneous entry.
  1. Dashboard KPI & Recent Submissions Overview: Upon signing in to /sk/dashboard, the system loads the SK Official's assigned Barangay profile, total My Evidence Uploads (Submitted to MYDO) count, and recent submission cards.
  2. Per-Activity Submission Status Check: Navigating to /sk/events-activities, the SK Official selects or inspects an activity card where the system evaluates my_evidences count:
    • If 0 Entries Uploaded: The card displays No evidence uploaded yet alongside a prompt button to upload event evidence.
    • If 1 or More Entries Uploaded: The card displays a green Evidence Uploaded (N Entries) status badge confirming the report is shared with MYDO Staff.
  3. Inspect Gallery & Follow-Up Action: Clicking View / Upload More Evidence opens the modal displaying the submitted photo gallery, narrative text, and exact submission timestamp. From here, the SK Official can either close the modal (keeping the report as-is) or confirm Delete on an erroneous entry (DELETE /sk/events-activities/{activity}/evidence/{evidence}), which verifies ownership, deletes the image files from storage, removes the database row, and refreshes the event's submission badge.
