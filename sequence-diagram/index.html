    Note over Admin, DB: 1. Governance, Categories, Programs & Role Setup
    Admin->>App: Configure Activity Categories, Scholarship Types & Programs
    App->>DB: Persist foundational records & write ActivityLog
    Staff->>App: Register SK Official with Position & Barangay Jurisdiction
    App->>DB: Create User (role=sk, sk_position, provCode, citymunCode, brgyCode)

    Note over Youth, Staff: 2. Youth Registration & Staff Vetting Gate
    Youth->>App: Submit Online Registration + School ID upload
    App->>DB: Save YouthProfile (status=pending, is_active=false, ref=YD-XXXX-XXXX)
    Staff->>App: Review & Approve Youth Registration
    App->>DB: Update YouthProfile (status=approved, is_active=true)

    Note over Youth, Services: 3. Youth Services & Real-Time Notifications
    Youth->>App: Apply for Scholarship (COE, COG, ID, Cedula, PSA)
    App->>DB: Create ScholarshipApplication (SCH-XXXX) + Staff DB Notifications
    App-->>Services: Broadcast NewScholarshipApplicationEvent to Staff Bell
    Staff->>App: Evaluate Dossier & Approve / Reject Application
    App->>DB: Update status + write ActivityLog + Youth DB Notification
    App-->>Services: Broadcast ScholarshipApplicationStatusEvent to Youth Bell

    Note over Admin, Youth: 4. Events, QR Check-In, SK Evidence & Cash Incentives
    Youth->>App: Join Published Activity & generate QR Event Pass
    Staff->>App: Scan Youth QR / Manual Code at Venue Check-in Terminal
    App->>DB: Lock rows & create ActivityAttendance
    SK->>App: Upload Barangay Event Pictures & Narrative Evidence
    App->>DB: Store SkActivityEvidence linked to SK Official & Barangay
    Admin->>App: Audit Attendance, Cash Incentive Releases & Activity Logs
