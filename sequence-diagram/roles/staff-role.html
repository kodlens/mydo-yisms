Staff Flow 01

Youth Registration Vetting & SK Official Barangay Registration

Staff inspect newly registered Youth profiles (including uploaded School IDs) to approve or reject portal access, and register SK Official accounts assigned to specific Barangays.

StaffYouthProfileController StaffSkOfficialController ActivityLogger 
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Staff as MYDO Staff Officer
    participant UI as Staff Workspace UI
    participant YouthCtrl as StaffYouthProfileController
    participant SkCtrl as StaffSkOfficialController
    participant DB as MySQL (youth_profiles, users, activity_logs)

    Note over Staff, DB: 1. Youth Profile Review & Account Activation
    Staff->>UI: Open /staff/youth-profiles & filter status=pending
    UI->>YouthCtrl: GET /staff/get-youth-profiles
    YouthCtrl->>DB: Query YouthProfile with province, city, barangay & approver
    YouthCtrl-->>UI: Return paginated youth profiles + School ID preview path
    Staff->>UI: Inspect Youth Profile Drawer & click Approve or Reject
    alt Approve Youth Account
        UI->>YouthCtrl: POST /staff/youth-profiles/{id}/approve
        YouthCtrl->>DB: Update status=approved, is_active=true, approved_by=staff.id
        YouthCtrl->>DB: Write ActivityLog (youth_profile_approved)
        YouthCtrl-->>UI: Confirm account activated for portal login
    else Reject Youth Account
        UI->>YouthCtrl: POST /staff/youth-profiles/{id}/reject (rejection_reason)
        YouthCtrl->>DB: Update status=rejected, is_active=false, rejection_reason
        YouthCtrl->>DB: Write ActivityLog (youth_profile_rejected)
        YouthCtrl-->>UI: Confirm rejection saved
    end

    Note over Staff, DB: 2. SK Official Registration with Barangay Jurisdiction
    Staff->>UI: Open /staff/sk-officials & click Register SK Official
    UI->>UI: Select SK Position + cascading Province, City, Barangay
    UI->>SkCtrl: POST /staff/sk-officials (username, password, sk_position, provCode, citymunCode, brgyCode)
    SkCtrl->>DB: Insert User (role=sk, sk_position, provCode, citymunCode, brgyCode, street_address)
    SkCtrl->>DB: Write ActivityLog (sk_official.created with Brgy. Name)
    SkCtrl-->>UI: Return success & refresh SK Official directory
Staff Flow 02

Scholarships Management, Real-Time Pusher Alerts & Applicant Evaluation

Staff manage scholarship offerings, receive instant WebSocket notifications whenever a student submits an application, examine uploaded documents, and issue decisions that broadcast back to the student.

StaffScholarshipController StaffApplicantController StaffNotificationController Pusher / Laravel Echo 
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Staff as MYDO Staff Officer
    participant Bell as Staff UI + Notification Bell
    participant Ctrl as StaffScholarship & Applicant Controllers
    participant DB as MySQL (scholarships, applications, notifications)
    participant Pusher as Pusher WebSockets
    actor Youth as Youth Applicant

    Note over Staff, DB: 1. Scholarship Program Publishing
    Staff->>Bell: Create or toggle active status on /staff/scholarships
    Bell->>Ctrl: POST /staff/scholarships or /toggle-status
    Ctrl->>DB: Save ScholarshipType & log action

    Note over Youth, Bell: 2. Real-Time Incoming Application Notification
    Youth->>DB: Submit Scholarship Application
    Pusher-->>Bell: Push NewScholarshipApplicationEvent to private-staff.{id}
    Bell-->>Staff: Increment unread badge & append live notification item
    Staff->>Bell: Click notification item
    Bell->>Ctrl: POST /staff/notifications/{id}/read & navigate to /staff/applicants/{id}

    Note over Staff, Youth: 3. Dossier Verification & Status Broadcast
    Ctrl->>DB: Load ScholarshipApplication + YouthProfile + COE, COG, ID, Cedula, PSA
    Ctrl-->>Bell: Render Applicant Review Page
    Staff->>Bell: Verify GWA & documents, then click Approve or Reject
    Bell->>Ctrl: PATCH /staff/applicants/{applicant}/status
    Ctrl->>DB: Update status, reviewed_by, remarks & write ActivityLog
    Ctrl->>DB: Save ScholarshipApplicationStatusNotification for Youth
    Ctrl-->>Pusher: Broadcast ScholarshipApplicationStatusEvent to Youth channel
    Pusher-->>Youth: Real-time status alert delivered to Youth Bell
Staff Flow 03

Events, Live QR Check-In, Cash Incentives & CSV Analytics Reports

End-to-end operational execution for municipal events, venue QR attendance scanning, cash incentive disbursements, and multi-filter CSV report exports.

StaffEventActivityController StaffActivityAttendanceController StaffCashIncentive*Controllers StaffReportController 
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    actor Staff as MYDO Staff Officer
    participant UI as Staff Workspace UI
    participant EventAtt as Event & Attendance Controllers
    participant CashRep as Cash Incentive & Report Controllers
    participant DB as MySQL Database

    Note over Staff, DB: 1. Events, Sign-ups, SK Evidence & Live QR Check-In
    Staff->>UI: Create/Publish Activity & approve pending sign-ups
    UI->>EventAtt: POST /staff/events-activities & PATCH /participants/{reg}
    EventAtt->>DB: Save Activity & update ActivityRegistration status
    Staff->>UI: Open /staff/events-activities/{id}/attendance (Check-in Terminal)
    UI->>EventAtt: POST /attendance (scan QR or enter YD-XXXX-XXXX)
    EventAtt->>DB: ActivityAttendanceService locks rows & records check-in
    EventAtt-->>UI: Play confirmation chime & refresh live turnout metrics
    Staff->>UI: Open SK Evidence Modal on Events table
    UI->>EventAtt: GET /staff/events-activities/{id}/sk-evidences
    EventAtt-->>UI: Display SK Official photos, narrative & Brgy. badge

    Note over Staff, DB: 2. Cash Incentives Selection & Payout Recording
    Staff->>UI: Create Cash Incentive Program & select eligible youth
    UI->>CashRep: POST /staff/cash-incentives/{program}/beneficiaries/select
    CashRep->>DB: Snapshot youth academic/barangay data into beneficiaries
    Staff->>UI: Review beneficiaries & record individual or batch payouts
    UI->>CashRep: POST /releases/{beneficiary}/record or /releases/batch
    CashRep->>DB: Insert/Update CashIncentiveRelease (status=released) + history

    Note over Staff, DB: 3. Comprehensive MYDO Staff Reports & CSV Export
    Staff->>UI: Open /staff/reports, filter by Program & Year, click Export CSV
    UI->>CashRep: GET /staff/reports/export?type=barangay&year=...
    CashRep->>DB: Aggregate barangay, scholarship, event & incentive metrics
    CashRep-->>Staff: Stream downloadable CSV report file
